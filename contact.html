Host

Iman Mossavat

Hello and welcome! Deep Dives with Iman is a podcast about artificial intelligence and what it means for people and society. In each episode I talk at length with one or two guests: researchers, engineers, legal and ethics experts, founders, educators and artists. It is for curious listeners, students, professionals and policy makers. No prior AI knowledge is needed: the conversations go deep, but we explain things from the ground up and connect them to what matters today and for our future as a society.

Some of the questions we look into:

  • Why is it so hard for a robot to learn a skill and perform it reliably on real hardware? (Episode 32: Jens Kober)
  • What happens inside a language model, and can its knowledge be surgically edited? (Episode 31: Jelle Zuidema)
  • How should software engineering education adapt when LLMs write code in seconds? (Episode 30: Alexander Serebrenik and Lina Ochoa)
  • Can large language models stand in for human participants in research? (Episode 29: Benjamin Paaßen)
  • How can AI help organisations build an institutional memory from their own knowledge? (Episode 28: Jakub Zavrel)
  • Why did early AI work on protein structure prediction go largely unnoticed, and what does that say about academia and Big Tech? (Episode 27: Daniel Cremers)
  • What comes after LSTM, and can smaller, efficient models like TiRex compete with the giants? (Episode 26: Sepp Hochreiter)
  • How do we tell real media from synthetic when seeing isn't always believing? (Episode 25: Tina Nikoukhah)
  • Can probability theory help explain how the brain perceives the world? (Episode 24: Hadi Vafaii)
  • How do well-meaning leaders end up at the heart of corporate scandals, and can AI deepen ethical blindness? (Episode 23: Guido Palazzo)
  • Why do AI systems often fail silently, and how can auditing surface hidden harms? (Episode 22: Shea Brown)
  • Can school-placement algorithms be efficient, fair and transparent all at once? (Episode 21: Mayesha Tasnim and Paul Verhagen)
  • How can geometry and topology help with real problems, from epidemic modelling to medical imaging? (Episode 20: Colleen Farrelly)
  • How can mathematics be taught so that no one is left behind? (Episode 19: Luis Serrano)
  • How is AI shaping the future of creative industry? (Episode 15: Mei-Li Nieuwland, illustrator, graphic journalist, cultural anthropologist)
  • Could Generative AI be Useful for Science and Understanding the World? (Episode 14: Jakub Tomczak)
  • How can nature inspire artificial intelligence research to revolutionize energy efficiency? (Episode 18: Yulia Sandamirskaya and episode 12 Federico Corradi)
  • Why doctors outperform AI in healthcare realistic scenarios? (Episode 10: Artin Entezarjou)
  • What if we focused on balance rather than maximization narrow objectives in AI design? (Episode 9: Diederik Roijers)
  • How can philosophy, psychology, and law inform AI design? (Episode 8: Mehdi Dastani)
  • What does it take to make real-time decisions in high-stakes environments and how to deal with uncertainty? (Episode 7: Albert Podusenko and İsmail Şenöz)
  • What are AI's ethical and legal challenges? (Episode 4 with Colette Cuijpers, episode 6 with Inge Brattinga, episode 13 with Ger Janssen, episode 17 with Tara Steele)
  • How ideas from macroevolution, extinction, and competition inform our understanding of AI? (Episode 5 with Indre Žliobaitė)
  • Why can't current AI truly understand cause and effect and how to fix that? (Episode 3: Alexander Molak)

Join me as I discuss innovation, human-centered design, and the future of technology with experts, scientists, and leaders.
