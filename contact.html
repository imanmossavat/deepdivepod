Hello and welcome! Deep Dives with Iman explores artificial intelligence and what it means for people and society. In each episode, I have in-depth conversations with researchers, engineers, legal and ethics experts, founders, educators and artists.

The podcast is for curious listeners, students, professionals and policymakers. No prior knowledge of AI is required. We explore complex ideas from the ground up and connect them to the questions that matter for our lives and our future.

Curious about the subjects we explore? Visit the topics page to discover the range of topics covered in the podcast.

I’m Iman Mossavat, an electrical engineer by training. I spent about a decade in industry as a senior design engineer, architect and researcher at ASML, working on inverse problems, probabilistic inference and deep learning for optical metrology. This work led to 14 granted US patents.

Since 2022, I have taught machine learning, statistics and data science at Fontys University of Applied Sciences in Eindhoven. You can find my publications on Google Scholar.

The first season (31 episodes) was published on the Radio 4 Brainport Spotify channel. From Season 2 onward, new episodes are published on our own Spotify channel and through our RSS feed.
